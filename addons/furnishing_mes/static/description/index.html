Furnishing MES

A Manufacturing Execution System for furnishing production — capacity-aware planning, shop-floor output capture, downtime analysis, maintenance and management dashboards, built on Odoo 18 Community.

What it replaces

Manual Excel production planning, paper downtime logs, and month-end guesswork about machine utilisation. Every figure lands in one centralised database the moment it happens on the shop floor.

Production Planning

Plans generated from a machine capacity matrix, allowing for shift hours, machine availability and the manpower actually rostered — machine-wise and shift-wise, without a spreadsheet.

Shop-Floor Terminal

A touch-first tablet screen for operators: start and stop work orders, record output and rejects, and log downtime with a coded reason — in one flow, tolerant of unreliable shop-floor Wi-Fi.

Utilisation & OEE

Machine utilisation, standard versus actual output, bottleneck and under-utilisation detection — built on Odoo's native OEE computation rather than a parallel one.

Downtime Analysis

Every stoppage carries a coded loss reason, so Pareto analysis shows exactly where production hours are going.

Maintenance

Preventive schedules raised automatically before they fall due, breakdowns linked to the downtime that triggered them, and MTBF / MTTR health tracking per machine.

Dashboards & Reports

Achievement, utilisation, downtime, backlog and maintenance KPIs on one board, plus a full suite of scheduled PDF and Excel reports.

Roles

Role Access
Operator Shop-Floor Terminal only — their allocated machines, output and downtime
Supervisor Shift scheduling, approvals, maintenance escalation, operator allocation
Plant Manager Full control, configuration and executive MIS
Customer Portal — own order progress and support tickets

Extends Odoo's native Manufacturing and Maintenance apps rather than duplicating them, so native OEE, MTBF and MTTR stay correct. Runs entirely on Odoo 18 Community — no Enterprise module is required. ERP integration is designed for but not yet enabled.
