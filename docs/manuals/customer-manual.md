# Customer Manual

For customers with portal access — tracking your own orders and raising
support tickets. Your access is deliberately small: you see only your own
company's data, nothing about how the plant is run internally (no machines,
costs, or downtime).

---

## 1. Getting Access

You cannot sign yourself up. Your plant contact grants you portal access from
their side; you'll receive an email invitation with a link to set your own
password. Once set, log in at the plant's portal address with your email and
that password.

## 2. Your Home Page (`/my`)

After logging in you land on your portal home, showing two tiles: your open
**Orders** and open **Tickets**. Click either to see the list.

## 3. Tracking an Order

**My Orders** lists every sales order for your company. Open one to see:

- The order lines and quantities, as usual
- A **Production Progress** section per line: quantity produced so far,
  progress %, and expected completion date

This is pulled directly from the plant's production system, so it reflects
real shop-floor progress, not a manually updated estimate. You do not see
which machine is running your order, its cost, or any downtime affecting
it — only your own progress and expected date.

## 4. Support Tickets

- **My Tickets** lists tickets raised by anyone at your company (not just
  you personally) — so a colleague's ticket is visible to you too.
- **Raise a new ticket**: fill in the subject and description; it's created
  under your name and company.
- **Reply to a ticket**: open it and use the message box at the bottom — this
  goes straight to the plant's support team, and their replies appear in the
  same thread.
- Ticket status (open / in progress / resolved / closed) is shown on both the
  list and the detail page.

## 5. Privacy

You can only ever see your own company's orders and tickets — trying to open
another customer's record (for example, by guessing a link) redirects you
back to your home page rather than showing anything. There is no way to
reach the plant's internal system from your portal login.
